## 资格（这一段决定了我们只能报一个类）

> "For all categories except Best Speculative Ad, entries must meet all of the following:
> **01 Commercially Commissioned Work (Required)** — The project must have been commissioned by a real brand, client, organisation, or product owner."

我们是独立创作者，没有品牌委托 → **21 个类别里只有 08 Best Speculative Ad 可报**。
该类原文：*"The work must take the form of a compelling, commercially relevant ad for a real brand."*
→ 允许对真实品牌做未受委托的 spec 广告。
