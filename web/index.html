The idea

你喝到的，是三十年前的雪。

What you taste is snow from thirty years ago.

Changbai Snow comes from a cold spring inside the Changbai Mountains. The water fell as snow, seeped into volcanic basalt, and travelled through the rock for thirty to sixty years before it surfaced at a constant 7 °C. That number is on the brand's own product page.

It is also a number nobody has ever seen. For eight years the brand's films have stayed outside the mountain — snow, deer, forest, four seasons, mist. Beautiful, and always from the outside.

So this film goes in. Eight shots, one direction of travel, two camera moves: a very slow push, and a very slow pull. No people. No voice-over. No music.

The product's entire value is a duration. The category's entire visual language is landscape. Show the thirty years, not the mountain.

The brand's real line closes the film, unchanged:

我们不生产水，
我们只是大自然的搬运工。

We don't produce water. We are just the porters of nature.

The build

A 30-second ad has no room for a shot that drifts.

So the film is built like software: one source file, five build stages, four gates, and a command that fails the build when the film and its documents disagree.

  • One source file. Eight shots, camera curves, three supers, the end card and ten sound cues live in film.py. The shot list is generated from it; the build fails if the document drifts.
  • Seven stills, not seventy. Motion is procedural: sub-pixel moves with smoothstep easing, a cold grade, 0.75 % grain, 28 % vignette — identical on every rebuild.
  • Sound without music. Wind, rock, water and one 110 Hz bell, synthesised from noise and modal partials. Normalised to −14 LUFS, true peak −2.2 dBTP.
  • 25 assertions before the first frame. Timeline adds to 720 frames; cuts are cuts; every super sits inside a 5 % safe area; the palette stays cold; every glyph exists in the bundled font.
  • A fresh clone reproduces it. The verifier re-renders eight frames from the delivered file and requires ≥45 dB PSNR against a from-scratch render.
  • Why AI, specifically. The thirty years happen inside rock — no light, no space, no second camera position. Generation made the impossible shot the cheapest shot, not the fastest one.
